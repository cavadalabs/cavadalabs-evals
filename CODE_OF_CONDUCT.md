# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We welcome constructive participation regardless of identity, background,
experience, disability, nationality, or viewpoint. Be respectful, discuss the
work rather than the person, disclose relevant conflicts of interest, and make
technical claims with reproducible evidence.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of
private information, credential or holdout disclosure, and knowingly misleading
benchmark claims are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project.
Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions must be documented
privately, proportionate, and free from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the official repository's security
advisory channel. Do not include secrets, personal data, private benchmark cases,
or exploit details in a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially to the
extent practical and lawful.

## Benchmark-specific integrity

Contributors must not conceal cherry-picking, modify released evaluation data,
misrepresent development results as official, identify blinded systems or
reviewers, or submit data without the necessary rights and consent. Good-faith
methodology criticism and responsible disclosure are protected contributions.
